Legal Text Example:

    Terms of Service (ToS): Add a clause in your Terms of Service document:

        "Unauthorized access, including but not limited to hacking, password mining, or other means of accessing unauthorized information, is strictly prohibited and may result in civil and/or criminal liability. We reserve the right to take legal action against individuals or entities involved in such activities."

    Disclaimer or Warning Banner: Display a visible disclaimer on your website:

        "Unauthorized access or attempts to disrupt this service are violations of the law and may lead to prosecution under applicable laws, including the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (CFAA). All activities are logged and monitored."

    Privacy Policy: State in your Privacy Policy that:

        "We take the security of our systems seriously. Attempts to breach security measures may be reported to the appropriate authorities."

Where to Place It:

    Terms of Service Page: Include a page/route dedicated to prohibited activities.
    Footer of Your Website: Add a link to the full legal terms or display a condensed version as a notice.
    Login or Registration Page: Include a brief note about unauthorized access.
    Contact Page or Info: Display a security notice for ethical hackers and vulnerability disclosures.
